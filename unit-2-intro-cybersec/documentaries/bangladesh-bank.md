4) Stolen SWIFT credential made fake transfers look fully legitimate the confirmation printer was hacked and disabled so staff wouldn’t see the paper trail delaying detection matters as much as evading it once money moves attackers just need enough time to launder it before it can be clawed back.

5) A misspelling tipped off a routing bank freezing 850M which shows that luck played a huge role and that even skilled attackers make careless mistakes while existing safeguards worked despite the banks own defense failing.
